How It Works

Phantom Identity runs before any tracking scripts can load on a webpage. It intercepts browser APIs and randomizes identifying information like Canvas fingerprints, WebGL data, User-Agent strings, and screen properties. Additionally, it simulates natural human behavior to make your browsing patterns indistinguishable from real users. All processing happens locally - your data never leaves your device.
